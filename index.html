The rules every line was checked against

The owner set these on 9–11 Nov, with Sky's notes from 10 Nov. Words that broke them are struck through in the “before” columns.

No Odoo or ERPNot in ad text, assets or keywords. Odoo Partner is Singapore only.
Sell to the accountantNo “call”, no Day 1–5 list, no Lean/Agile. Say “15 to 20 minute online meeting”.
No fixed price, no “free”Drop “Fixed Written Quote” and “Free Adviser Call”.
“5 Day” with a qualifier“As quick as 5 days, depending on scope”. No hyphen in “5-Day” or “30-minute”.
Vague titles name BIR CASEvery headline, callout and sitelink title says BIR CAS. Proof figures may stay as they are.
CAS first, long-tail onlyNo e-invoicing focus and no short two-word keywords. “Not affiliated with BIR” stays.
